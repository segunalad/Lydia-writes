01 — About

The writer behind the byline

I'm Lydia Oladejo, a creative writer working across content, scriptwriting, and copy. I bring wit and strategic thinking to every brief — turning ideas into narratives that hold attention and move people to act.

From film scripts and TV commercials to editorial features and brand campaigns, my range covers the page and the screen. Whatever the format, the aim is the same: writing that reads effortlessly and does a job.

I combine the instincts of a storyteller with the eye of an editor and the mindset of a content strategist. Lydia Oladejo
